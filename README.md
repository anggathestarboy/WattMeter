# Wattmeter

**Wattmeter** is an open-source mobile application designed to monitor and track the battery condition of your device in real time. It provides useful battery information to help users understand their device's battery performance, charging activity, and overall battery health.

## Features

- **Real-Time Battery Monitoring** — Monitor your device's battery level in real time.
- **Battery Health Tracking** — Track battery health and condition.
- **Charging Status** — Monitor whether your device is charging, discharging, or fully charged.
- **Battery Temperature** — View the current battery temperature.
- **Voltage Monitoring** — Display battery voltage information.
- **Charging Information** — Track battery charging activity and related statistics.

## Overview

Wattmeter aims to provide a simple and informative way to monitor your device's battery condition. With an intuitive interface, users can easily access important battery metrics and better understand how their device's battery performs over time.
